I am a Master's student in Computer Technology at [Harbin Institute of Technology](https://www.hit.edu.cn/), where I am fortunate to be supported by the Xiaomi Scholarship. Previously, I received my B.Sc. in Mathematics and Applied Mathematics from [South China University of Technology](https://www.scut.edu.cn/), supported by the Australian Alumni Scholarship.

My research focuses on **multimodal world models** and **spatial intelligence**—developing structured, verifiable, and actionable world representations from sparse, dynamic, and incomplete visual-language observations. I am particularly interested in:

- **Multimodal spatial reasoning**: learning robust 3D/4D scene representations through VLM priors, geometric verification, and uncertainty-aware active evidence selection.
- **Geometry-aware generation**: developing controllable visual generators that maintain geometric, physical, and temporal consistency under novel views and dynamic conditions.
- **World-model-driven agents**: connecting perception and generation with planning, post-training, and interaction feedback for vision-language-action (VLA) agents.

I am preparing applications for PhD programs beginning in **2027**, and I welcome conversations about research collaboration and doctoral opportunities.
